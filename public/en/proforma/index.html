To prepare your project

Your team supplies branding, ES/EN copy, project information and approved drawings with dimensions, elevations and material references. No existing 3D model is required. Scope and terms

One project, one landing page and one exterior building model. Excludes interiors, apartment selection, live inventory, CRM, WhatsApp automation, hosting, domain and third-party licences. Includes source-code handover. A personalised proforma is valid for 15 days; extras are quoted and approved separately.

Delivery and reviews

We agree on the modelling and development schedule after reviewing drawings, before the deposit. Includes 2 consolidated review rounds within scope. Work starts with the deposit and all approved materials; the schedule pauses while awaiting feedback or materials.

Payment and support

50% deposit and 50% after delivery approval, before launch, based on the total of the accepted quote. In-scope bug fixes for 30 days after launch; new features and content updates are quoted separately.
